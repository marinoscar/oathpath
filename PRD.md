## Executive Summary

### Purpose of OathPath

**OathPath is an AI-powered naturalization preparation companion designed to help people move from uncertainty about the U.S. citizenship process to genuine confidence and readiness for their naturalization interview and test.**

Preparing for naturalization is not simply a matter of memorizing civics questions. A user must learn information, retain it over time, recall it without prompts, understand questions when they are spoken, respond verbally in English when required, become comfortable interacting with an interviewer, practice reading and writing where applicable, and ultimately perform under the pressure of a real USCIS interview.

Traditional study materials are valuable sources of information, but information alone does not create readiness.

That gap is why we are building OathPath.

The fundamental user problem OathPath is intended to solve is:

> **“How do I know that I am actually ready for my citizenship interview?”**

A person can read every question, watch videos, use flashcards, or perform well on multiple-choice quizzes and still feel uncertain about what will happen when sitting across from a USCIS officer and being asked questions aloud.

OathPath must help close the gap between **knowing the material and being ready to perform**.

The product therefore transforms naturalization preparation from a collection of study activities into a guided journey. OathPath continuously helps the user understand:

> **Where am I in my preparation?**  
> **What should I practice next?**  
> **What am I struggling with?**  
> **Am I retaining what I learned?**  
> **Can I answer the questions aloud?**  
> **How have I improved?**  
> **What still stands between me and being ready?**

The intended progression is:

**Uncertain → Oriented → Learning → Remembering → Speaking → Practicing → Performing → Ready**

OathPath should never create artificial confidence after a few successful questions. Readiness must develop over time and be supported by evidence from repeated practice, retention, spoken responses, realistic mock interviews, reading and writing exercises where applicable, and consistent performance.

The product's core promise is therefore not:

> **“We will help you memorize the citizenship questions.”**

It is:

> **“We will help you prepare until the interview feels familiar and you have evidence that you are ready.”**

---

### Why the Name OathPath

The name **OathPath** intentionally represents both the destination and the journey.

The naturalization process ultimately culminates in one of its most meaningful moments: the **Oath of Allegiance**, when an applicant completes the naturalization process and becomes a United States citizen.

The word **Oath** represents that destination.

The word **Path** represents everything required to reach it.

Naturalization preparation does not happen in a single study session. Users may spend weeks or months learning, forgetting, reviewing, practicing English, answering questions aloud, improving weak areas, taking mock interviews, building confidence, and preparing themselves emotionally for the real experience.

OathPath exists to accompany the user throughout that process.

The name therefore expresses the fundamental product philosophy:

> **The oath is the destination. OathPath helps the user navigate the path that leads there.**

This journey concept must extend beyond branding. It should influence the application's information architecture, visual design, readiness system, AI behavior, notifications, progress experience, milestones, gamification, and daily recommendations.

Users should feel that they are progressing along a meaningful path rather than merely completing an endless bank of questions.

OathPath should continually reinforce:

> **You do not need to become ready today. You need to keep moving toward ready.**

That distinction is critical to the emotional experience of the product.

### Target User

The primary OathPath user is **a person preparing for the U.S. naturalization process who wants to become knowledgeable, comfortable, confident, and genuinely prepared for the citizenship interview and test.**

The target user may be at almost any stage of preparation.

They may have recently submitted Form N-400 and have months before an interview.

They may already have an interview scheduled and feel pressure to prepare quickly.

They may have studied independently but have no objective sense of whether they are ready.

They may know many civics answers when reading them but struggle to recall those answers without seeing choices.

They may understand written English more comfortably than spoken English.

They may know an answer but become nervous when asked unexpectedly.

They may feel uncomfortable practicing English with another person because they are worried about making mistakes.

They may have studied the questions extensively but never experienced anything resembling a real naturalization interview.

They may simply be asking:

> **“Am I ready?”**

OathPath must be designed for that user.

Many users will also be immigrants for whom English is not their first language. The experience must therefore be patient, culturally respectful, accessible, and supportive without becoming simplistic or patronizing.

The product must recognize that difficulty answering a question can have several causes:

- the user does not yet know the information;
- the user learned it previously but cannot retrieve it;
- the user understands the concept but struggles to express it in English;
- the user knows the answer but did not understand the spoken question;
- speech recognition misunderstood the response;
- the user knows the answer but becomes less confident under interview conditions.

OathPath should help distinguish these situations and respond appropriately.

The user should never feel judged for needing repetition, asking for an explanation, practicing the same question many times, using another language to understand a concept, or struggling to answer verbally.

At the same time, OathPath must not provide false reassurance.

The AI companion should be encouraging because the user is making meaningful progress—not simply because encouragement increases engagement.

### The OathPath Product Experience

OathPath should not be designed as a traditional citizenship study application with an AI chatbot added to it.

It should be designed as an **AI-guided preparation journey in which intelligence is embedded throughout the product experience**.

The AI companion should understand the user's preparation history, recent performance, strengths, weaknesses, study preferences, readiness evidence, and current stage of the journey.

It should help determine the next best action.

Some days that may mean learning new civics questions.

Another day it may mean reviewing material the user is beginning to forget.

Another may require practicing answers aloud.

Another may focus almost entirely on a weak topic.

Eventually, the AI should recommend increasingly realistic interview simulations.

The relationship should feel continuous:

> **“Yesterday we worked on these questions. You remembered four of the five today. Let's reinforce the one that is still giving you trouble.”**

rather than transactional:

> **“Select another quiz.”**

Users must be able to interact naturally through **text, voice, or a seamless combination of both**.

Voice is particularly important because the naturalization interview is fundamentally a human conversation. OathPath must help users progress from recognizing answers on a screen to comfortably hearing and answering questions aloud.

The AI companion should therefore serve several roles throughout the journey:

**Teacher** — explain unfamiliar concepts clearly.

**Study Coach** — guide daily practice and recommend what to work on next.

**Practice Partner** — ask questions conversationally through voice or text.

**Encourager** — recognize meaningful progress and help users recover from setbacks.

**Evaluator** — assess whether responses meaningfully match authoritative accepted answers.

**Interview Simulator** — conduct realistic, neutral mock USCIS interview experiences.

**Progress Guide** — explain how the user's preparation is evolving and what is preventing a higher level of readiness.

These roles should feel like one coherent companion experience even when implemented through different underlying AI agents and deterministic product services.

### Readiness as the Core Product Outcome

The central product metric presented to the user should be **Readiness**, not simply questions completed or quizzes passed.

OathPath should maintain a continuously evolving **Readiness Score** based on evidence accumulated across the user's journey.

Readiness should reflect signals such as:

- breadth of official material studied;
- ability to recall answers without hints;
- repeated success across multiple days;
- retention over time;
- spoken-answer practice;
- performance in realistic mock interviews;
- remediation of previously weak questions;
- reading and writing practice where applicable;
- consistency of recent performance;
- confidence calibration;
- sufficient evidence across different preparation activities.

A user who correctly answers ten questions immediately after studying them should not appear highly ready.

A user who has demonstrated strong recall across the full material, retained that knowledge over time, practiced speaking extensively, addressed weak areas, and repeatedly performed well in realistic simulations has much stronger evidence of readiness.

The application must communicate this distinction clearly.

OathPath should therefore be able to tell a user not only:

> **Readiness: 72**

but also:

> **Your civics knowledge is strong, but you have limited interview practice. Completing two mock interviews is the best way to strengthen your readiness now.**

Every readiness measurement should answer the next question:

> **“What should I do next?”**

---

### Engagement and the Journey

Naturalization preparation requires consistency, so OathPath must make returning to practice easy and rewarding.

The product should use thoughtful gamification, streaks, milestones, achievements, daily goals, personalized challenges, intelligent reminders, progress celebrations, and re-engagement mechanisms to help users maintain momentum.

However, engagement mechanics are not the same as readiness.

OathPath must maintain a strict separation between:

**Engagement:**  
*Am I consistently doing the work?*

and

**Readiness:**  
*Does the evidence indicate that I am becoming prepared?*

Points, streaks, achievements, and challenges encourage the journey.

They must never artificially increase the user's Readiness Score.

The desired daily experience is:

**Open OathPath → immediately understand where you are → receive a clear recommended activity → practice for a manageable amount of time → receive useful feedback → see evidence of progress → understand what comes next → leave feeling more capable than when you arrived.**

Repeated hundreds of times across the preparation journey, that loop becomes the core of the OathPath experience.

---

### Trust and Accuracy

Because users may rely heavily on OathPath when preparing for a high-stakes government interview, accuracy must take priority over AI creativity.

The fundamental architecture and product rule is:

> **OathPath owns the truth. AI owns the interaction.**

Official USCIS questions, accepted answers, applicable test versions, current-official answers, and other authoritative facts must come from OathPath's verified, versioned content and data systems.

AI should then use that trusted information to:

- teach;
- explain;
- converse;
- personalize;
- evaluate;
- simulate;
- motivate;
- and recommend.

The AI must not independently invent official questions or determine authoritative answers from model memory when OathPath can provide verified data.

This distinction allows OathPath to deliver the natural conversational experience users expect from modern AI while maintaining the accuracy and trust required for naturalization preparation.

---

### Product Vision

OathPath succeeds when a user enters the naturalization interview not because an application told them to feel confident, but because they have spent weeks building evidence that gives them a reason to be confident.

They have heard the questions.

They have recalled the answers.

They have spoken them aloud.

They have forgotten some and learned them again.

They have practiced when nervous.

They have experienced realistic interviews.

They know their weak areas.

They have watched those weaknesses become strengths.

And the interview no longer feels completely unfamiliar.

That is the experience OathPath exists to create.

> **OathPath helps people travel the path from uncertainty to readiness—and ultimately toward the oath they have been working to reach.**
