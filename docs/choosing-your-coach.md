# Choosing Your Coach

OathPath doesn't just tell you right or wrong — it talks to you about it.
After most answers, and whenever it explains one, you're hearing from a
coach with a particular voice. You get to choose which one.

This page explains the four voices, what changing them does and doesn't
do, and where to make the choice.

## The one thing to know before anything else

**Your coach's voice never changes your score, your readiness, or what
counts as a correct answer.** Every persona reads the same verdict off the
same answer. Switching voices changes how something is said to you — never
what is true about your answer, and never how ready OathPath says you are
for your interview.

## The four voices

**Supportive** — Warm, specific, and honest. Encouragement you have
actually earned, never cheerleading. This is the default, and it is how
OathPath has always spoken.

**Academic** — Precise and formal. Names the concept and the distinction
you missed, and explains rather than reassures.

**Playful** — Light and quick, with a sense of humour. Jokes about a wrong
answer, celebrates a right one, and never lingers.

**Unfiltered** — Blunt and irreverent. It will call a weak answer weak and
joke about it. It never gets personal — the target is always the answer,
never you. Pick this one only if you want a coach that softens nothing.

## Supportive is the default, and changes nothing

If you never open this setting, you're already using it. Supportive isn't
a new voice added alongside the one OathPath always had — it *is* the one
OathPath always had, given a name. Nothing about your practice changes
unless you go looking for something different.

## Unfiltered is blunt on purpose

Unfiltered exists for learners who find gentler coaching a little flat and
want something with more edge. Blunt means direct about the answer: it
will say plainly that a response missed, without softening it or padding
it with praise it doesn't mean.

Blunt does not mean personal. Whatever you choose, your coach never
comments on your English, your accent, your background, or your prospects
of becoming a citizen — and it never implies you're slow or that the
material should be obvious to you. The target of any joke is always the
miss, never you. A wrong answer always ends by pointing you toward what to
do next, not by leaving you stuck on the miss itself.

If that isn't the coach you want, Supportive, Academic, or Playful are
right there, and switching costs you nothing.

## Turning reactions off

Beyond changing the voice, you can turn off the short reaction line that
appears after an answer entirely, if you'd rather just see the verdict.
Explanations still work the way you'd expect either way — this only
affects the quick line after a practice attempt.

## Where to find it

Go to **Settings → Coach** to pick a voice, read a sample line from each
before you choose, and turn reactions on or off. Whatever you pick, you
can change it again any time — there's no commitment and no confirmation
step, just your choice, updated the moment you make it.
